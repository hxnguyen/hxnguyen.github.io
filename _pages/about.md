I am a Professor and Academic Lead for Cybersecurity and Networking in the School of Computer Science and Information Technology at Adelaide University. I lead the Cyber-AI Research Group, which develops autonomous and robust methods for securing AI agents, enterprise networks, and cyber-physical systems.

I currently serve as Theme Lead for Information Warfare and Advanced Cyber within the Defence Trailblazer program, a joint initiative between Adelaide University and UNSW that concludes in late 2026. My Defence Trailblazer activities have focused on translating cybersecurity research into capabilities developed with defence and industry partners.

My research develops autonomous and provably robust methods for defending complex networked systems. Current themes include AI agent security, structural defence of enterprise networks, and edge-AI protection of cyber-physical and operational technology environments. My group studies agent poisoning (memories, documents, skills and tools), agent misbehaviour and alignment, interpretable agent actions, Active Directory attack graphs, and assurance across OT/IT boundaries.

I serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence and Elsevier Ad Hoc Networks. I am  a member of the Technical Program Committees for leading international conferences on AI and cybersecurity, including AAMAS 2026 (**CORE A\***), AAAI 2026 (**CORE A\***), The Web Conference 2026 (**CORE A\***), and IJCAI 2026 (**CORE A\***).
